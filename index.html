Why Get a Label Maker?

Label makers aren't just for Type-A organizers anymore. They're essential tools for home organization, small businesses, shipping, pantry labeling, file management, and crafting. A good label maker pays for itself in time saved — no more guessing what's in that storage bin or rewriting faded Sharpie labels. Modern label makers connect to your phone via Bluetooth, print in multiple fonts and sizes, and cost as little as $20.

Quick Guide: Which Type Do You Need?

  • Home organization: Handheld or Bluetooth label maker ($20-$60)
  • Office/filing: Desktop label maker with keyboard ($30-$80)
  • Shipping/business: Thermal label printer ($80-$200)
  • Crafting/decorative: Bluetooth with app for custom designs ($30-$80)
