10 Best Label Makers 2026

From simple home organizers to professional shipping printers. Updated May 2026.

We've researched every label maker worth considering in 2026 — comparing print quality, tape costs, ease of use, and long-term value. Whether you're organizing your pantry or shipping 100 packages a day, here are the 10 best options.

#1 Best Overall

Brother P-Touch PTD220

★★★★★ 4.7/5
$30–$40

The Brother PTD220 has been the best-selling label maker for years, and for good reason. It's simple, reliable, and produces professional-looking laminated labels that last forever. The built-in keyboard with a small screen lets you preview labels before printing — no phone app required. It uses Brother's TZe tape which is waterproof, heat-resistant, and won't fade. At $30-40, it's the best value in label making.

★★★★☆ 4.5/5
